Biography

I am a third-year Ph.D. student advised by Prof. Guangyu Zhou at the School of Psychological and Cognitive Sciences, Peking University. My research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence and clinical psychology, with a particular focus on health psychology.

I am interested in how computational methods, large language models, and digital interventions can support more valid, ethical, and context-sensitive mental health research and care.
